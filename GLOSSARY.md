# YouTube Transcriber

A personal tool for obtaining readable text from one YouTube video at a time.

## Language

**Video link**:
The YouTube URL identifying the single video whose transcript is requested.

**Transcript**:
A readable text representation of a video's spoken content in its original language, arranged into paragraphs. The copied transcript contains only transcript text, without timestamps or video metadata.
_Avoid_: Summary (a condensed account rather than the spoken content)

**Captions**:
Existing text accompanying a YouTube video, either supplied by its creator or automatically generated. Captions are the source for the first version's transcript.

**Transcribe**:
The user's action to request a transcript from existing captions for the supplied video link.
